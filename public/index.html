TRANSPARENT DATA CONNECTIONS

Retailer integrations
without the guesswork.

PriceWise only labels a retailer as connected after an authorised product source responds. This page shows exactly what is configured, what is missing, and what access each retailer requires.

How to connect a retailer

Add an entry to PRICEWISE_FEEDS in your .env file using the documented feed contract, then set the credential variables named on the retailer card. PriceWise never scrapes retailer websites, bypasses access controls, or invents product data.

Some retailers do not offer a public product API. In that case the retailer stays visibly unconnected until you obtain authorised affiliate or partner access and a compatible feed.

THE PRICEWISE PROMISE

Better comparisons
start with better data.

PriceWise is built to help Indian shoppers compare like-for-like products without confusing old prices, lookalikes, or unsupported claims for real deals.

01

Authorised sources only

Offers come from retailer APIs and feeds the operator is authorised to use. We do not scrape stores or bypass access controls.

02

Prices with context

Every offer shows its source or fetch time and freshness. “Verified” requires a source-reported in-stock status from a recent authorised fetch.

03

Careful product matching

Listings are grouped only when they share a product identifier or an explicit canonical match key, including the exact variant.

04

Direct store links

Buy Now uses the exact product URL supplied by the configured feed and is restricted to the feed's approved retailer domains.

About our data

When no valid authorised retailer feed is configured, PriceWise shows no products and no placeholder prices. Add your Flipkart Affiliate API credentials to load real listings. When feeds are configured, only offers returned by those feeds are displayed, including when a feed fails or returns no listings.

Retailer names, images, prices, stock status and product links are displayed only when supplied by an authorised source. Unknown delivery charges, seller details or timestamps are shown as unknown rather than guessed.

PriceWise is an independent comparison tool and is not affiliated with the retailers shown. Retailer trademarks remain the property of their respective owners. Always confirm the final price, delivery costs and availability on the retailer's website before buying.
